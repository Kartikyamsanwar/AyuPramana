## Recording and merging (Windows, free)

1. **Record the voice:** close the door, hold the phone or mic about 15 cm away, and read one block at a time. Windows
   Sound Recorder or your phone's recorder works.
2. **Merge in Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11, Start → Clipchamp):
   1. New video → import `AyuPramana_demo_trimmed.mp4` and your audio file(s).
   2. Drag the video to the timeline, then drag each voice block under it at the start time shown above.
   3. Optional: add soft background music at about 10% volume.
   4. Export → **1080p**.
3. **Upload** to YouTube as **Unlisted** and paste the link in the SIH form.
